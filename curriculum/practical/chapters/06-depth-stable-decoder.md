## Formula

For positive complete-block count $L$, apply $\alpha=1/\sqrt L$ once to
`blocks.{b}.attention.output.weight` and `blocks.{b}.ffn.down.weight` for every
block. Two residual additions per block do not change the divisor to $2L$.
Q/K/V, gate/up, embeddings and their tied vocabulary head, and norm gains are
unselected. Store transformed weights; forward adds no second multiplier.
Those initialized stored values can subsequently evolve normally.

For ideal Xavier uniform initialization, a $4\times4$ matrix has half-bound
$\sqrt{3/4}$ and variance $1/4$. The selected depth-two and depth-eight
distributions have half-bounds $\sqrt{3/8}$ and $\sqrt{3/32}$, and variances
$1/8$ and $1/32$. Keep ideal distribution reasoning separate from finite `f64`
samples and the illustrative coefficient vector.

In the toy sum of $2L$ independent, zero-mean increments with common variance
$\sigma^2$, scaling each increment gives variance
$2L\alpha^2\sigma^2=2\sigma^2$. State every assumption. Real residual
branches and their Jacobians are correlated; this is no general stability proof.
For $y=2w_{\mathrm{stored}}$, the stored-weight derivative is $2$. Through
$w_{\mathrm{stored}}=\alpha w_{\mathrm{base}}$, the base-coordinate derivative
is $2\alpha$. No optimizer-equivalence or convergence guarantee follows.

## Rust behavior

`FreshDepthInitialization::prepare` validates a positive-depth policy and the
existing configuration's checked census against caller parameter and position
bounds before allocating model arrays. It clones the caller RNG, calls
`DecoderModel::new` once and checks the complete existing parameter layout.
`complete` consumes preparation and first refuses parameter updates or nonzero
accumulated gradients before transforming weights or committing RNG. Constructor
zero gradients and `zero_grad` are allowed when parameters have not been updated.
For a valid fresh preparation, it stages selected transforms, reconstructs
through `DecoderModel::from_parameters` and then commits RNG. Dropping preparation
or any refusal leaves the caller RNG unchanged. One block bypasses transformation
and rebuilding, preserving parameter bits, names, shapes, order, RNG consumption,
forward/loss values and parameter adjoints. Legacy zero-block construction exists;
the new policy refuses zero.

Existing or restored states are explicit refused inputs to fresh initialization.
`DepthInitializedDecoder::from_initialized_parameters` accepts the typed
construction record and actual stored values, restores those values through the
existing constructor and never scales again. The record contains construction
provenance, not optimizer state or a trained-checkpoint serialization format.

`observe_residual_health` measures input, attention-addition and FFN-addition
residual handles in forward order, counting shared boundaries once: $2L+1$ sites.
RMS is $\sqrt{\sum x^2/(nd)}$ for $n$ valid positions and $d$ features. Gather
valid entries before overflow-safe reduction; never use NaN-times-zero padding
arithmetic. RMS is not centered variance, and RMSNorm does not establish centered
unit variance.

The existing reverse engine completes the valid-target mean cross entropy in
nats; the observer selects fully accumulated adjoints by exact node identity at
its finish boundary. It adds no graph operations, random draws or weight changes.
Activation ratios use $A_0$; adjoint ratios use $G_{2L}$, the terminal residual
before final RMSNorm and the tied head whose downstream operations contribute to
that adjoint. Refuse zero/nonfinite normalizers without epsilon repair. Compare
finite unrounded ratios inclusively with $[0.01,100]$. Report branch-output RMS
and norm gains separately, outside this classification.
