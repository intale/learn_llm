The forward/backward scale argument assumes independent zero-centered weights, independent zero-centered input features, and zero-centered arriving gradients, each group with its own common variance. Weights must be independent of the inputs and arriving gradients, and the activation is assumed symmetric and near-linear with unit slope. The target belongs to the sampling distribution; finite samples need not match it exactly. Nonlinearities, normalization, residual paths, depth, data, and optimization affect actual decoder signals, so no exact decoder-signal variance is guaranteed.
