# Isolated-surface criteria

Assess every supplied unit as its complete frozen isolation boundary and in its
stated actual role. Repeat the frozen role requirement exactly; do not invent,
narrow or replace it, split a legitimately grouped unit, or assume unavailable
surrounding material.

Check coherent English, grammar, technical register, explicit referents, actors,
operations, conditions, comparisons, quantities, units, mappings, scope, actions
and destinations required by that role. Standalone copy must carry its necessary
referents. Contextual headings and genuinely associated groups need not repeat
the whole lesson or every limitation beyond their frozen role requirement.

Judge accessibility meaning from the supplied words and language-neutral
literals, not unseen styling, colors, position, interface chrome or presumed
author intent. An accessible description must state the relationships needed
for its stated purpose; numerical values alone may not explain them.

Give a distinct substantive rationale for every unit. Apply the schema's
severity and verdict rules consistently. Do not infer a suspected correction or
expected outcome from a unit's identifier. A clean pass is valid; do not invent
findings or an expected verdict. Return judgments only, not revised copy.
