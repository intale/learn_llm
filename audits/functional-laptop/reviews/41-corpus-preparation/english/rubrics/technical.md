# Technical and pedagogical criteria

Assess every supplied complete document, reading-order unit and isolated surface
against its frozen role requirement, supplied evidence and commitment map.
Repeat each requirement exactly in its assessment; do not narrow or replace it.

Check technical and mathematical truth, executable evidence, numerical units and
denominators, assumptions, conditions, actors, operations, causal relationships,
ordering, scope, limitations and excluded inferences. Distinguish observations,
derivations, course-local policies, proposals and future measurements. Copied
claims or matching projections are not independent executable evidence.

Check agreement among source, built content, formulas, Rust excerpts, recorded
outputs, diagrams, exercises, answers and handoffs. Judge whether explanations
make the required relationships understandable at the stated learner level.
Respect the declared revision scope without ignoring inconsistencies introduced
by the revision. Preserved material must not acquire a different meaning through
changed context.

Compare complete source and built documents with the inventory. Report omitted
or misclassified affected atoms, reading units, isolation groups and role
requirements against their owning complete-document surfaces. Check that each
frozen requirement covers the evidence-backed commitments for its actual role.
An audit-only extraction view provides exact values and provenance, not an
additional learner-facing source of context or proof.

Give each unit a substantive evidence-based rationale. Report unresolved source
ambiguity as a blocker rather than selecting a plausible interpretation. Apply
the output schema's severity and verdict rules consistently. A clean pass is
valid; do not invent findings or an expected verdict.
