The identity’s symbols and fixed settings R_{\mathrm{ref}} is a 256-bit digest, displayed as 64 lowercase hexadecimal characters. Its three named inputs are: \mathrm{config}_{1188}: a canonical record of eighteen tiny-run settings, together with the invariant that the decoder has 1188 parameters. \mathrm{fixture}: a canonical record of SHA-256 digests of the exact corpus bytes and exact split-manifest bytes. Even a byte change that leaves parsed JSON meaning unchanged creates a different input identity. \mathrm{source\_revision}: a verified source identity combining a baseline revision with the exact forty course-owned scalar-reference source files. Here \Vert means composition into the shared identity format’s named, canonical fields, not writing three unframed strings beside one another. That distinction matters: without field boundaries, different input sequences can have the same concatenated bytes. The helper orders fields, serializes one unambiguous record and hashes its complete bytes. The settings fix eight BPE merges; decoder width, feed-forward width and context capacity of four; one attention head and one block; stride one; training and evaluation batch sizes of sixteen and one hundred twenty-eight; thirty-two updates; learning rate 0.04; gradient-norm cap 1; training seed 39; generation seed 38; bigram smoothing 1; generation temperature 0.8; top-k four; and three generated tokens. The identity records floating-point settings by their exact bit patterns rather than rounded display text. It does not prove that the algorithm is correct or that a new machine will reproduce every floating-point bit. The source identity deliberately covers the forty scalar-reference source files, not this chapter’s reporting wrapper or interface. Toolchain, locked dependencies and arithmetic environment are recorded separately. A matching source digest is therefore not evidence that two execution environments match. The temporary checkpoint path is also outside the input identity; changing that path does not change the model’s fixed inputs. Likewise, exact checkpoint reload is narrower than complete job resumption. This check does not restore every data cursor, in-flight operation or whole-job continuation state. No laptop throughput is measured here.
