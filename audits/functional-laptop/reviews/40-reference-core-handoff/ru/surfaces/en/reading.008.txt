Explain why constructing an identity for changed inputs does not admit them to the fixed reference run. Explain why a successful checkpoint round trip cannot justify the statement “the complete training job can resume,” and why 1744 must not be labeled “distinct test transitions.” Answers: input identity, evidence scope and repeated target slots The digest binds inputs; replay and reload compare actual behavior. Neither kind of evidence substitutes for the other. The target-count basis explicitly derives 2048 targets per schedule and 4096 for primary plus replay. The reported mean NLL uses nats per overlapping window-target slot. The pure mutation tests construct different identities without calling the pipeline. The runner separately requires the exact accepted corpus and split bytes, so an identity is not an admission permit. The checkpoint check covers persisted model, optimizer and tokenizer state plus a fixed logit probe, not every whole-job continuation dependency. The 1744 denominator counts overlapping window-target slots; the underlying documents contain 442 transition occurrences.
