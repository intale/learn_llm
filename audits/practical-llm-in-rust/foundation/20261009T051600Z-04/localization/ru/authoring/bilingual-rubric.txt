# Bilingual home-chooser review requirements

Read all 19 supplied surfaces, first as complete documents and genuine reading
units, then at each contextual-heading, independent-action and catalog-value
role. Judge the nine new Russian values directly against their supplied English
values. Check meaning, technical terminology, explicit actors and referents,
order, scope, prerequisites, accessible names and natural technical Russian.
Do not invent or narrow the requirements below. A contextual heading need not
repeat its associated card. The card paragraphs form a genuine reading group;
do not import unrelated material to rescue an independent action or catalog
value. A canonical-English ambiguity affecting these requirements is a source
blocker, not permission to repair the source in Russian.

Course purposes describe what the learner will study; they must not become
claims that later practical work or measured improvements have already been
completed. The practical course extends the completed Rust model, begins with
an orientation Chapter 0, and is available in English. Its prerequisite has
two alternatives: the first course's basics, or equivalent experience covering
both Rust and LLMs. The first course covers Chapters 0–39.
